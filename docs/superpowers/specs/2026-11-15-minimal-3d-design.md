# Minimal 3D: keep each piece, cut to one idea

## Why
The site carried four busy 3D pieces. Rings, particles and orbits filled every
scene, and the S logo appeared three times. Sudi wants it to feel minimal while
keeping the Blender work.

## Goal
Each piece says one thing. Non-goals: no new effects beyond those listed here,
and no copy changes. Anti-goal: a page where decoration competes with the
projects and the name.

## Approaches
1. **Keep each piece, stripped to one idea.** Chosen by Sudi.
2. Only the hero stays 3D, and Featured Projects goes back to plain cards. Sudi
   later chose this for Featured Projects (see below).

## The design, as settled with Sudi during the work
- **Hero (cosmos.glb):**
  - **Kept:** the yin-yang with its rim, raised a little; the five planets with
    their labels; a dense spiral galaxy and starfield (Sudi asked for more stars).
    The galaxy fades into the page at the canvas edges.
  - **The S:** stands at the centre beneath the yin-yang, always turning to face
    the viewer, with a gentle sway. Its finish is iridescent chrome with rounded
    edges and a soft halo, and an "I am" label points to it: the centre the five
    reflections turn around.
  - **Removed:** the gyroscope rings, the planets' moons and rings, the orbiting
    S, and the ground rings and wave contours.
- **Featured Projects:** cards only, no 3D. The Blender showcase is removed.
- **Menu bar S:** stays 3D, with the same chrome finish as the hero S.
- **Work page warp:**
  - **Kept:** the star streaks and the project portals.
  - **Removed:** the tunnel rings and the rings around each portal.
  - **Part of the page, not a card:** the canvas is transparent over the page's
    own background in both themes and fades out at its edges, and the text and
    rail use the theme colours.
  - **Picked, not scrolled:** the page scrolls normally past the warp. A
    vertical stepper of numbered dots (horizontal on phones) picks a project,
    and the camera flies to it. The skip links are gone.
  - **The only list:** while the warp runs, the plain project list is hidden.
    It still shows under reduced motion, without WebGL, or before the warp
    loads. Each stop links to the live site and the source (pill buttons).
  - **Galaxies:** seven small spiral galaxies drift past the portals.
- **About page career:** "Where I've Worked" is a stepper. The current role is
  marked "Now", and the line fills and each dot lights up as the reader scrolls
  back through the years.
- **More galaxies in the hero:** eight small far galaxies sit behind the scene,
  around the edges of the camera's view, clear of the labels.
