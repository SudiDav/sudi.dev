Waves Ressources is a mining services company in the DRC working the whole value chain — exploration and due diligence at one end, transformation and commercialisation at the other, with extraction, logistics, compliance and supply chain in between.

WavesOps is the platform that runs it. Its central surface is a **Control Room**, and access is role-based: what you can see and do comes from your account rather than from which page you can reach.

That constraint is the interesting one. A mining operation is not one team — it is geologists, site operations, logistics, compliance officers and finance, and most of them should not see most of each other's work. Traceability and regulatory compliance are explicit parts of the business, which means the audit trail is a feature rather than a by-product.
