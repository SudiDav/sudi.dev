I am currently leading the redevelopment of a school management system used by Danish schools — replacing a working, in-use platform rather than building on a blank page.

We started with **Absence**, which sounds like the simplest module and is not. Attendance is where the school's legal obligations, the parents' expectations and the teachers' actual morning routine all meet. Get it wrong and someone finds out quickly.

Rewriting a system that is already in service sets the constraints. The old one keeps running while the new one takes over in pieces, so each module has to stand on its own and still agree with whatever it has not replaced yet.

The team is distributed across regions and time zones, which puts a premium on decisions being written down rather than discussed. Most of what makes this work is not technical.
