A car auction platform built as a way to work through microservices patterns properly rather than reading about them.

Services communicate asynchronously over RabbitMQ rather than calling each other directly, so a slow or failed service degrades the system instead of breaking it. Each service is containerised with Docker and the whole thing runs on Kubernetes.

The interesting part was not the auction logic — it was everything around it: how services find each other, what happens to a bid when a consumer is down, and how to keep the data consistent when there is no single database to lean on.
