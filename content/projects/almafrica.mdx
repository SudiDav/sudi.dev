Almafrica works on inclusive agricultural value chains — the phrase does a lot of quiet work. A value chain is everyone between the person who grows something and the person who eventually eats it: buyers, processors, transporters, lenders. "Inclusive" means the smallholder farmer at the start of it is supposed to capture a fair share, rather than being the one part of the chain with no leverage and no records.

Software matters here for an unglamorous reason. A farmer with no documented history of what they grew, sold and were paid is a farmer no lender can assess. The record is the thing that unlocks the financing.

I am building and consulting on the platform behind that.
