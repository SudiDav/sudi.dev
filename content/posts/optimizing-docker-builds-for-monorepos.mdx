Our monorepo's Docker builds had crept up to twelve minutes per service, and most of that time was spent reinstalling dependencies that hadn't changed. The fix wasn't a single trick but a combination of small, disciplined changes.

Multi-stage builds let us separate the dependency-installation layer from the application-code layer, so Docker's cache could actually do its job. Pairing that with BuildKit's remote cache export meant CI runners could share cache across branches instead of starting cold every time.

The result: build times dropped from twelve minutes to under four, and the pattern was simple enough that every team in the monorepo could adopt it without a rewrite.
