Event sourcing promises a complete, auditable history of everything that ever happened in a system — which is exactly what a fintech application needs when every state change has to be explainable months later.

The tradeoff nobody warns you about upfront is the operational cost: rebuilding read models from an event stream at scale takes real engineering effort, and event schema changes require the same discipline as any other long-lived contract, since old events never go away.

Two years in, the audit trail has paid for itself many times over during compliance reviews. But I'd tell anyone starting out: invest in snapshotting and projection tooling from day one, not after your event stream gets too large to replay quickly.
